This Policy explains which personal data Everni processes, for what purposes, with whom it is shared and how you can exercise your rights, in accordance with Brazil’s General Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, LGPD). It forms part of Everni’s Terms of Use.

01Who We Are

Everni is a subscription management app for Shopify stores, operated by L.V.M. Alexandrino, registered under Brazilian CNPJ no. 32.086.364/0001-22, headquartered at Rua Dom Barreto, 692, Paraíso, Americana, São Paulo, Brazil, ZIP 13465-720.

Regarding the data of the stores’ end customers, Everni acts as processor and the merchant is the controller. Merchants’ own account data and the data of visitors to this website are processed by Everni as controller.

02Data We Collect

Merchant data
Name, email, Shopify store information and billing data required to provide the service.
End-customer data
Name, email, shipping address, order and subscription history, and the information required for recurring billing. We do not store full card numbers: they are processed directly by the payment gateways.
Technical data
Access logs, IP address, device identifiers and usage data, used for security and service improvement.
Website visitors
To show this website in the right language, your browser queries an IP geolocation service (api.country.is or, as a fallback, ipapi.co) that returns only your country. We store in your browser (local storage, not cookies) the country code for up to 30 days and the language you choose. This data is not sent to Everni. If you sign up for updates, we process the email you provide.

03How We Use Data

We use data to:

  • operate subscriptions and recurring billing;
  • provide the subscriber self-service portal;
  • generate reports and analytics for the merchant;
  • provide support;
  • ensure security and prevent fraud;
  • show this website in the language suited to the visitor’s country;
  • send updates to people who signed up, with the option to unsubscribe at any time;
  • comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

04Legal Bases (LGPD)

We process personal data based on performance of a contract, compliance with legal or regulatory obligations, legitimate interest (for security, service improvement and choosing the website language) and consent where required (such as signing up for updates).

Where the applicable basis is the end customers’ consent, the merchant is responsible for obtaining it.

05Sharing

We share data only as needed to operate the service, with:

  • Shopify;
  • payment gateways and processors;
  • cloud infrastructure and website hosting providers (GitHub Pages);
  • IP geolocation services used by the website (api.country.is and ipapi.co), which receive the visitor’s IP address;
  • communication and form tools.

These subprocessors take on contractual data protection obligations consistent with this Policy.

06International Transfers

Some servers and subprocessors, including website hosting and the geolocation services, may be located outside Brazil. In such cases, we adopt the safeguards provided for in the LGPD, such as standard contractual clauses and guarantees of an adequate level of protection.

07Data Retention

We keep data for as long as needed to fulfil the purposes of this Policy and legal obligations.

After the app is uninstalled, the merchant’s and their customers’ data are deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law. We also handle customer and shop data deletion requests received through Shopify, as required by the platform.

On this website, the detected country stays in your browser for up to 30 days; you can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

08Security

We adopt technical and organizational measures to protect data, such as encryption in transit and at rest, access control, monitoring and audits. This website is served over HTTPS only.

No system is completely immune to failures, but we manage risks continuously.

09Your Rights

Under the LGPD, you may request:

  • confirmation that processing takes place and access to the data;
  • correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
  • anonymization, blocking or deletion of data;
  • data portability;
  • information about who the data is shared with;
  • withdrawal of consent.

To exercise your rights, contact our data protection officer (section 12). End customers of the stores should first contact the merchant, who is the controller of their data; Everni supports the merchant whenever needed. We respond within the legal deadlines.

10Cookies, Local Storage and Language

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising. It only uses your browser’s local storage to remember the language you chose and the detected country, as described in section 2. To choose the language manually, use the PT/EN switch at the top of the page.

The Everni admin uses only essential cookies, required for authentication and operation. The widget shown in the store follows the store’s own cookie settings.

11Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by email or within the app.

The last updated date at the top of this page shows the version in force. This English version is provided for convenience; in case of conflict, the Portuguese version prevails.
