How much does Subbly cost?

There are three tiers, based on monthly subscription GMV: from $0 to $500k, 1% of GMV; from $500k to $2.5M, 0.75%; above $2.5M, 0.5%. There's no fixed monthly fee — the app is free to install and you only pay the percentage of the month's GMV. Billing is handled by Shopify, in USD.

Do I need to change my theme or hire a developer?

No. The subscription widget automatically inherits your theme's design, and plans are created on a single screen, with no code.

Can I migrate my subscribers from another platform?

Yes. Migration is assisted and has no downtime, including subscribers coming from Recharge. Your team just follows along.

What can subscribers do on their own?

In the customer portal they can pause, skip, postpone or cancel, swap products and update payment details without opening a ticket.

What happens when a customer's card is declined?

Subbly retries automatically and sends the customer straight to the portal to update their card, recovering revenue that would otherwise be lost.

Does it work for B2B?

Yes. You can set up recurring restocks, scheduled orders and contracts with minimum and maximum cycles, billed to corporate cards.

What currency am I billed in?

The app fee is billed in USD through Shopify Billing.
