How it works

Everything a subscription needs, in five parts.

Subscription plans

Create plans by product, frequency and discount. Customers choose to subscribe on the product page and complete the purchase in your store’s checkout.

Recurring billing

Every cycle, Everni creates the order and charges the subscriber automatically. Upcoming charges and renewals live in a single list.

Retention

When a payment is declined, Everni retries and notifies the customer. Pause, skip and product swap offer alternatives before cancellation.

Subscriber portal

In the portal, subscribers skip a delivery, pause, swap products or update their card without contacting support.

Reports

Monthly recurring revenue (MRR), retained revenue and cohort retention, with unit and period on every number.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Everni?

Everni is a native Shopify app for subscriptions and recurring billing. It handles subscription plans, automated billing, failed payment recovery, a self-serve customer portal and recurring revenue analytics for B2C, D2C and B2B brands.

Does Everni run inside Shopify?

Yes. Plans, billing and payments run natively on Shopify, and your subscribers buy through your store’s checkout.

How much does Everni cost?

Free to install, then 1% of monthly subscription GMV, with no monthly fee and no per-order fee; billed in USD through Shopify. High-volume brands can talk to us about Enterprise, with negotiated rates.

What does Enterprise add?

Everything in Growth, plus 24/7 support, a dedicated success manager, direct input on the roadmap, early access to new features, custom development and volume pricing.

How does the 30-day pilot work?

You install Everni, set up your plans and bring your active subscribers. For the first 30 days, migration is free and our team runs the operation alongside you.

Can I bring subscribers from another app?

Yes. We migrate subscribers from Recharge, Skio, other apps or spreadsheets. When payment details are already stored in Shopify, subscribers carry on without re-entering their card; otherwise, each subscriber gets a link to update it.

What happens when a payment is declined?

Everni retries the charge on scheduled dates and notifies the subscriber with a link to update their card. Pause, skip and product swap are available in the portal before cancellation.

Which payment gateways does Everni support?

Shopify Payments, Stripe, PayPal and Authorize.net today, through Shopify’s native subscription engine. More gateways are in the works; talk to us to prioritize yours.
